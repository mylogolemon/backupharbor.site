Effective date: 9 November 2026. By using BackupHarbor you understand that information is presented for general educational purposes.

No recovery guarantee

Backup outcomes vary by device, software version, encryption, storage health and account access. We cannot inspect your data or guarantee the success of a restore. Backups and software settings should be verified directly through official product documentation.

Avoid destructive procedures

Do not erase, reset, reformat, overwrite or physically modify a device because a general article suggested a concept. If important information may be at immediate risk, seek qualified help first. Our practice exercises deliberately recommend using harmless sample files and separate restore locations.
