ABOUT THIS PUBLICATION

Editorial principles

How we write and update independent backup and restore explainers.

Useful before impressive

Articles begin with a specific reader problem, not a trending keyword. We prioritize clear procedures, failure scenarios, trade-offs, verification steps and risks that can help readers protect their own information. We do not publish synthetic testimonials, made-up benchmarks or unverified claims about software vulnerabilities.

Primary-source checks

Platform-specific instructions are cross-checked with published documentation from the vendor. The source directory also includes CISA material for general backup resilience. A cited source supports only the relevant claim; the site's suggested workflow may be our independent synthesis of those references.

Updates and corrections

Articles display a publication date. We update that date only when material editorial changes are made. Software menus, eligibility, prices and policies may change; readers should consult the linked current support article before performing a device-specific step. Send corrections with the article URL and supporting information to mylogolemon@gmail.com.

Independence and advertising

Our educational content is not sponsored by product vendors. Google advertising technology may be present; ads, if served, do not establish technical endorsement. Editorial recommendations are not based on advertiser payments. Read our advertising disclosure.

What we do not claim

We do not remotely scan devices, inspect your cloud account, recover lost files, or test backups on your behalf. The two planning tools are client-side checklists and do not establish that a copy is genuinely recoverable.
