ABOUT THIS PUBLICATION

Privacy policy

How website visits, Google ads and private browser-based tools may process information.

Effective date: 9 November 2026. BackupHarbor publishes general guides and offers optional tools that run in your web browser.

Information you provide

Reading guides does not require registration. The Backup Plan Builder processes selections in your browser; it does not upload files or submit data to a server. The Restore Drill Log can save checklist progress and notes to your browser's localStorage if you choose to save. You can delete that information through the tool's Reset control or your browser's site-data settings. Avoid entering passwords or private files.

Advertising and cookies

Pages with substantial editorial content include the Google AdSense script for site verification and potential ad serving. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, personalize and measure ads, subject to applicable settings and consent. Read Google's advertising technology explanation. We do not claim to control Google's separate processing.

Hosting and technical logs

The website is designed to be deployed through Vercel or a comparable static host. Hosting providers can record routine technical information such as IP address, user agent and request time for security and operations. We do not operate a custom user-account database.

Email communications

If you email us, your address and message will be processed through the email service for correspondence. Please do not share credentials, sensitive documents or backup files.

Consent requirements

Advertising or analytics deployments may require a certified consent-management solution or other notices in certain jurisdictions. These must be configured for the actual audience and publisher account before serving personalized ads; this page alone does not implement a consent manager.
