Recovery Lab

Hands-on experiments using disposable files, with expected evidence and safe stopping points.

Experiment 01 — Byte-for-byte verification

Download these three synthetic fixtures. Their SHA-256 values were computed from the distributed bytes during site preparation.

  1. Open File Integrity Checker. Compare original and identical. Expect MATCH.
  2. Compare original and altered. Expect DIFFERENT even though file sizes match.
  3. Use SHA-256 Generator to reproduce the digest table.

Experiment 02 — Safe restore rehearsal

Place original.txt in a disposable folder, copy it to a backup destination, and restore into a separate empty folder. Compare original and restored bytes. Open both files. Record the timestamp, elapsed time, source and result in Restore Drill Log. Do not overwrite your original.

Experiment 03 — Duplicate detection

Select all three samples in Duplicate File Finder. Expect one duplicate group containing original and identical. The altered sample is not a duplicate. Keep backup copies in independent locations.

Experiment 04 — Recovery bottleneck

In Recovery Time Calculator, enter 100 GB at 50 MB/s and 30 minutes setup. Expect 63.3 minutes. Halve the speed to see the impact. This is a model, not a measured transfer benchmark.

Evidence and limitations

These experiments are reproducible with synthetic files. They do not establish device-specific behavior or certify your complete backup. Open the restore guide for device-level verification.
