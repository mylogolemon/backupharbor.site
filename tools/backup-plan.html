How to read your result

The plan prioritizes independent recoverable copies, a suitable backup cadence and the ability to restore a file. A synchronized cloud folder may be useful, but its deletion and version rules should be verified. A disconnected drive reduces some shared risks; an unreadable encrypted drive is still not a successful backup.

If your documents change daily, a monthly copy can leave a substantial loss window. If your main concern is photos from occasional trips, a post-trip copy plus a regular check may be more practical. There is no single correct storage product for everyone.

Start with the 3-2-1 rule, then read backup versus sync and how to test a restore.
